# #ENTRYLEVELBOSS

Finished date: 2026/01/23
Author: Alexa Shoen
Language: English
Type: Ebook
Number of pages: 298
Notes: The #ENTRYLEVELBOSS Job Search System is an online education curriculum and methodology designed to transform "panicking job seekers" into "hired, happy professionals". Developed by Alexa Shoen, the system is described as a "fitness plan" for employment—a structured 9-step approach intended to reign in the chaos of the modern job market.
The system is built on the philosophy that job seekers must become the "CEO of their own career" rather than waiting for employers or academic institutions to direct their path. It is divided into four distinct phases:

Phase 1: Mental Reframing (Mindset Shift)
The system begins not with tactical applications, but by addressing the "internal panic" and "existential dread" that cause a job search to spin out of control.
• Challenging Assumptions: Part 1 focuses on debunking 14 incorrect beliefs about employment, such as the idea that a degree guarantees a job or that "official experience" is the only thing that counts.
• Humanizing the "Enemy": The system teaches that employers are not "faceless decision-makers" but imperfect humans who are often "making it up on the fly". This shift is designed to take the employer off a pedestal and allow for more natural, "platonically flirtatious" communication.

Phase 2: Preparation (Steps 1–5)
Before "battle," the system requires seekers to "sharpen their axe" through a series of foundational tasks.
• The Target Employer List (Step 1): Seekers must define a specific role, industry, and location to narrow their search radius.
• Skill Hacking (Step 2): Instead of seeking a new degree, the system encourages "hacking" one's skillset by identifying small, specific gaps and teaching oneself through online resources.
• Modern Collateral (Steps 3–5): This involves creating a "BuzzFeed-ified" CV that is highly skimmable and a cover letter that tells a compelling story. It also includes "finishing touches" like professional photos and LinkedIn optimization.

Phase 3: Strategic Networking (Steps 6–8)
The system posits that networking is the most effective way to secure a job, focusing on the "Theory of Weak Ties"—the idea that acquaintances and strangers are your most likely bridge-builders to new roles.
• Tiered Outreach: The system provides templates for reaching out to personal networks ("Board of Directors" emails), specific target companies, and "Magical Sparks" (strangers who inspire curiosity).
• Making it "Stupid-Easy": A core tenet is removing friction for the person being asked for help, such as providing specific time slots for meetings and always traveling to the contact's preferred location.

Phase 4: Execution and Habit (Step 9)
The final phase emphasizes that there is no "silver bullet"; success comes from "Rinse and Repeat".
• The Marketing Funnel: The system reframes the search as a marketing exercise. Seekers must consistently send high-quality "content" (outreach) to a high volume of people, accepting that only a small percentage will result in a "sale" or job offer.
• Dream Employee Behavior: The system concludes by teaching seekers to act and communicate like a "dream employee" before they are hired, including mastering the "Tell us about yourself" interview pitch and sending 24-hour thank-you notes.
Ultimately, the #ENTRYLEVELBOSS system is designed to "unstick" the job seeker, ensuring they always have a "next move" and can navigate the "particle theory" of the chaotic modern employment market.
