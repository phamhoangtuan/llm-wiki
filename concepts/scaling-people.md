## Summary

**Scaling People** is Claire Hughes Johnson's tactical framework for organizing chaos in fast-growing companies. The core message: "You can't build a strong house on weak soil." Before tackling systems, a manager must pass the Management Prerequisites Checklist and set the **performance metronome 10–20% faster** than the current limit — accepting that initial acceleration will produce errors, but driving profound improvement (source: [[sources/scaling-people-hughes-johnson]]).

## Four Essential Operating Principles

1. **Build self-awareness to build mutual awareness** — know your values, "Work with Me" preferences, strengths and weaknesses; you cannot build a complementary team if you don't know what capabilities you lack
2. **Say the thing you think you cannot say** — address the "it" (the issue) directly, not the "we" (relationship) or "I" (personal doubt); bring out the "stinky fish" immediately
3. **Distinguish management from leadership** — leaders set vision and direction; managers focus on human-centric execution; as managers become more senior, they must tackle adaptive problems (leadership) not just technical problems (management)
4. **Come back to your operating system** — rely on repeatable, common systems across all teams for consistency at scale

## The Four Core Frameworks

### CF1: Foundations and Planning for Goals and Resources

- **Founding Documents**: Mission, Long-Term Goals, Principles, Team Charters
- **Strategy**: "A strategy should hurt" — strong strategy demands painful trade-offs in resource allocation
- **Goals**: OKRs or SMART goals linking top-level priorities to team targets

### CF2: Comprehensive Hiring Approach

- **Candidate experience**: transparent job descriptions, well-defined processes
- **Hiring conversion funnel**: rigorous interviewing (maximize at ~8 people), rubrics for assessment
- **Intentional Onboarding**: structured integration for new hires
- **Hiring leaders**: treat as change management and acceptance-building to prevent "organ rejection" of new executives

### CF3: Intentional Team Development

- **Skill-will matrix**: diagnose whether issues stem from ability (skill) or motivation (will)
- **Restructuring**: explain by the strategic "why" to key people first; obsess about who needs to be in your "boat"
- **Rituals**: meetings with PAL (Purpose, Agenda, Limit); pass-downs for context sharing; documented meetings to prevent information asymmetry

### CF4: Feedback and Performance Mechanisms

- **"Feedback is a gift"** — manager's attitude must be one of service
- **Hypothesis-based coaching**: gather data → form theory about development area → test with the individual
- **Hard feedback**: act as "explorer, not lecturer" — seek mutual understanding
- **"No surprises"**: employee must be explicitly told they're not meeting expectations before any formal plan begins
- **Calibration**: managers compare assessments to ensure fairness and consistency
- **High performers**: coach "Pushers" (ambitious, critical, high-standards) and support "Pullers" (take on too much, risk burnout)

## The Ultimate Project: You

- **"Put your own oxygen mask on first"** — cultivate psychological strength and resilience
- **Own your trajectory**: keep a personal document tracking roles, skills acquired, and enjoyment; review every 6–18 months
- **Manage up and sideways**: advocate for your team and build organizational trust
