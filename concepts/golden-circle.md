## Summary

The **Golden Circle** is Simon Sinek's framework for how inspired leaders think, act, and communicate — from the *inside out*. Its three concentric layers are **WHY** (purpose, cause, belief), **HOW** (differentiating value proposition), and **WHAT** (products or functions). The central claim: **"People don't buy WHAT you do; they buy WHY you do it."** (source: [[sources/start-with-why-sinek]])

## The Three Layers

| Layer | Meaning | Who knows it |
| ----- | ------- | ------------ |
| **WHAT** | Products sold, job functions | Every organization |
| **HOW** | Differentiating process / value proposition | Some |
| **WHY** | Purpose, cause, belief — nothing to do with money | Very few |

Most organizations communicate *outside-in* (WHAT → WHY) because they move from the clearest to the fuzziest. Inspired leaders go *inside-out*.

## Biological Foundation

The circle maps to brain anatomy:

- **Neocortex** → WHAT: rational, analytical thought and language.
- **Limbic brain** → WHY and HOW: all feelings (trust, loyalty), and *all human decision-making* — but no capacity for language.

Because the decision-making brain cannot process language, people struggle to explain "gut decisions" and rationalize them with facts afterward.

## Manipulation vs Inspiration

| Manipulation | Inspiration |
| ------------ | ----------- |
| Price drops, promotions, fear, peer pressure, novelty | Acting from purpose and belonging |
| Drives short-term transactions | Creates loyalty — turning down a better product/price to stay with a brand |

## Sustaining the Why

- **The Split** — an org's greatest challenge is its own success: as it grows, WHY dilutes while WHAT swells.
- **The School Bus Test** — extract the founding vision into culture so it survives the leader's departure.
- **WHY/HOW partnerships** — visionary WHY-types need realist HOW-types to make vision tangible.
- **The Law of Diffusion** — reach innovators/early adopters first (left of the bell curve) to reach the tipping point.
- **The Celery Test** — adopt only practices/products consistent with your WHY.
- **Discovery, not invention** — a WHY is found by looking backward at founding purpose.
