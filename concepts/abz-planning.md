## Summary

**ABZ Planning** is Reid Hoffman's career-strategy framework from *The Start-Up of You*: treat your career like a start-up in "permanent beta," run concrete experiments, pivot when reality demands it, and always keep a safety net. It answers the question *"What should I do next?"* with action, iteration, and insurance (source: [[sources/the-start-up-of-you-hoffman]]).

## The Three Plans

| Plan | Role | Mindset |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Plan A** | Your current path — the hypothesis you're testing | Act first, iterate; failures must be **recoverable** |
| **Plan B** | A **pivot into an adjacent niche** — leveraging existing skills and relationships | Incremental moves, not radical reinvention |
| **Plan Z** | **Fallback safety net** — your baseline of survival and identity | Removes fear of failure so you can take bold risks |

## The Permanent Beta Mindset

- Treat your career as an ongoing, never-final product: **learn, adapt, evolve continuously**
- **Specialize, don't generalize** — excel in your strengths rather than chasing universal superiority
- **Align assets, aspirations, and market realities** — rebalance the three dynamically as they shift
- **Boost competitive advantage** — strengthen skills *or* position yourself where your assets outperform others

## Rules for Choosing

- **Act first, iterate** — prioritize actionable experiments with recoverable failures over analysis paralysis
- **Pivot adjacently** — transitions should exploit what you already have (skills, relationships)
- **Secure Plan Z** — the safety net is what makes Plan A risky-but-rational
- **Invest in relationships, not just networks** — quality connections, reciprocity, proactive maintenance
- **Synthesize critically** — filter advice, discard noise, weigh insights by relevance and credibility
