Staff+ Archetypes

Source: staff-engineers-pathUpdated: 2026-07-13
careerstaff-engineeringtechnical-leadershiproles

Summary

Staff+ Archetypes are the four primary "shapes" a Staff Engineer role can take, as defined by Tanya Reilly. Not all Staff roles look the same — understanding your archetype helps navigate ambiguity and align expectations. Most Staff+ engineers blend multiple archetypes and shift between them as organizational needs change.

The Four Archetypes

Archetype Primary Mode Signature Work
Tech Lead Executes with a team; bridges strategy and delivery Leading a critical project's technical direction while staying hands-on with the team's code and architecture
Architect Sets technical direction for a domain or system Writing design docs, reviewing architecture across teams, ensuring coherence across a complex system
Solver Tackles the company's hardest, most ambiguous problems Diving into a crisis no one understands, debugging across system boundaries, finding the root cause others missed
Right Hand Extends a senior leader's capacity with high autonomy Representing the CTO in meetings, drafting strategy documents, unblocking teams without formal authority

Archetype Fluidity

The same engineer may operate as:

  • Solver during an incident: diving deep, finding root cause, fixing it
  • Architect the next week: writing a design doc to prevent recurrence
  • Tech Lead for a quarter: driving a team through a migration
  • Right Hand periodically: helping VP Eng prepare board materials
The magic isn't in mastering one archetype — it's in knowing when to shift gears.

Four Disciplines

Archetypes map to four fluid disciplines that senior engineers move between:

Discipline Focus
Core Technical Design, code review, deep debugging
Product Thinking What to build, why, for whom
Project Management Sequencing, resourcing, risk mitigation
People Leadership Mentoring, influencing, building culture
