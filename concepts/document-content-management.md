# Document and Content Management

Managing the lifecycle of unstructured data. One of DAMA's eleven Knowledge Areas.

## Scope

Unlike structured database records, documents and content include:

- Documents (Word, PDF, spreadsheets)
- Emails and communications
- Images, video, and audio
- Web content
- Social media data

## Key Activities

- **Classification & Taxonomy**: Organizing content for discovery
- **Version Control**: Tracking document revisions and approvals
- **Retention & Disposal**: Applying lifecycle policies consistent with Data Governance
- **Search & Retrieval**: Making unstructured content findable
- **Records Management**: Legal and compliance requirements for official records

## Distinction from Data Management

Document management deals with *containers* (files, documents) while data management deals with *content* (values, records). The Knowledge Area bridges both perspectives.

## Role in DAMA Framework

Shares characteristics with both Data Storage (managing assets) and Data Governance (applying policies). Content management systems increasingly blur with data platforms as unstructured data becomes analytically valuable.
