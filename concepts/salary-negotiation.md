## Summary

**Salary Negotiation** is the preparation-heavy discipline of getting paid what you're worth — estimating your market value, setting a walk-away number, navigating the offer conversation, and justifying raises and promotions with evidence. Josh Doody's *Fearless Salary Negotiation* is the canonical playbook (source: [[sources/fearless-salary-negotiation-doody]]).

## Market Value Estimation

- **Three research channels**: online (salary sites, surveys), inter-company (what other firms pay), and intra-company (what your firm pays for the role)
- Find the **midpoint** of salary ranges; assess how you compare to the average
- Decide your **minimum salary requirement** (walk-away number) *before* any conversation — it is your benchmark for success

## Interview & Negotiation Strategy

- The interview has a **dual purpose**: sell yourself *and* assess whether the job/company is right for you
- **Avoid salary disclosure** (current or desired); reframe: *"I'd prefer to focus on the value I can bring rather than my current salary."*
- **Negotiate base salary only** — bonuses and stock are unpredictable in value
- A good **counter** is **10–20% above** the initial offer
- Phases: **Preparation → Post-interview follow-up → Initial offer → Counter → Prep for final discussion → Final discussion**

## Raises & Promotions

- A raise is justified when your **value has increased** since your salary was last set
- Companies promote on **proven results, not potential** — build the evidence
- If denied: ask your manager for a **plan to reach the goal**, then develop the needed skills

## Mindset & Communication

- **Walk-away confidence**: ending without a job beats an underpaid acceptance
- Confidence comes from **preparing your case** and knowing your worth
- Use **non-confrontational, value-focused language** in every salary discussion
