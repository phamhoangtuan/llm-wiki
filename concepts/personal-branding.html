# Personal Branding

The deliberate cultivation of a professional identity and presence. From #ENTRYLEVELBOSS, personal branding is the set of "modern collateral" that precedes and amplifies networking efforts.

Components

  • CV/Resume: "BuzzFeed-ified" — highly skimmable, visually scannable, results-oriented
  • Cover Letter: A compelling story, not a restatement of the CV
  • LinkedIn Profile: Optimized for search, aligned with the CV narrative
  • Professional Photos: Consistent, approachable visual identity
  • Online Presence: GitHub, portfolio, writing — evidence of competence before the interview

The Skimmability Principle

Hiring managers spend ~7 seconds on an initial CV scan. Your collateral must communicate value in that window:

  • Bullet points, not paragraphs
  • Numbers and results, not responsibilities
  • Visual hierarchy guides the eye to key information

Narrative Consistency

All collateral should tell one coherent story:

  • LinkedIn matches CV matches interview answers
  • The story is: "Here's the problem I solve, here's evidence I solve it well, here's why I'm looking for you"

Beyond Documents

Personal branding extends to behavior:

  • How you communicate in outreach emails
  • How you handle rejection and follow-up
  • The "dream employee" behaviors demonstrated before being hired (24-hour thank-you notes, prepared questions, follow-through)
