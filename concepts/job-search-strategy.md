# Job Search Strategy

A structured approach to job seeking that treats the search as a marketing exercise rather than a passive application process. Based on the #ENTRYLEVELBOSS system by Alexa Shoen.

## The CEO Mindset

The foundational philosophy: job seekers must become the "CEO of their own career" rather than waiting for employers or institutions to direct their path. This means:

- Proactively defining targets, not just responding to postings
- Treating the search as a full-stack marketing campaign
- Taking ownership of outcomes rather than blaming "the market"

## The Four-Phase Framework

1. **Mental Reframing**: Debunk false beliefs (degree guarantees job, official experience is all that counts). Humanize employers as imperfect people "making it up on the fly."

2. **Preparation**: Define target employer list, "skill hack" by closing small specific gaps via self-learning, create skimmable CV and compelling cover letters.

3. **Strategic Networking**: Leverage the Theory of Weak Ties — acquaintances are more likely bridge-builders than close friends. Tiered outreach to personal networks, target companies, and "Magical Sparks."

4. **Execution and Habit**: Reframe the search as a marketing funnel. High-volume, high-quality outreach with acceptance that only a small percentage converts. Always have a "next move."

## The Marketing Funnel Model

```
Outreach volume → Response rate → Interviews → Offers
      100             10%             10           2
```

The system emphasizes that rejection at any stage is normal — the math works if volume is maintained.

## "Stupid-Easy" Principle

Remove all friction for contacts: provide specific time slots, travel to their location, make the ask small and clear. The easier you make it to help, the more likely they will.
