## Summary

Shawn "Swyx" Wang's *The Coding Career Handbook* (413 pages) is a career-design playbook for software engineers, blending technical rigor with self-awareness, empathy, and intentional career design. The reader's notes condense eight guidelines on ownership, readability, writing, humility, iteration, negotiation, public learning, and tool choice.

## The Eight Guidelines

1. **Demonstrate proactive ownership** — act as a senior by anticipating problems, mentoring others, and taking responsibility — even without the title
2. **Prioritize code readability** — write code for humans first: avoid clever tricks, delay abstractions until patterns emerge, simplify maintenance
3. **Clarify ideas through writing** — use writing (docs, notes, RFCs) to refine thinking, advocate for solutions, and improve verbal communication
4. **Stay humble and adaptable** — acknowledge gaps in knowledge, learn from *everyone*, treat others as *they* want to be treated (Platinum Rule)
5. **Balance progress over perfection** — aim for "good enough" outcomes to avoid stagnation; ship iteratively and refine later
6. **Negotiate fearlessly, align with values** — negotiate job offers confidently (without fear of rescission) and prioritize roles aligned with your passions
7. **Learn publicly and continuously** — share experiments, clone projects, engage in discussions, and follow thought leaders to accelerate growth
8. **Choose tools wisely** — avoid overengineering; adopt tools only when contextually beneficial, not just because they're trendy
