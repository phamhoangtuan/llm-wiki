## Summary

Josh Doody's *Fearless Salary Negotiation* (286 pages) is a step-by-step, preparation-heavy playbook for negotiating a job offer — and for justifying raises and promotions once you're in a role. The book's core stance: negotiation is decided before it starts, by research and by a **walk-away number** you commit to in advance.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Salary Research & Market Value Estimation
- Estimate market value with **online, inter-company, and intra-company research**
- Find the midpoint of salary ranges; assess how you compare to the average
- Decide your **minimum salary requirement** (walk-away number) before negotiating — this is your benchmark for success

### 2. Interview & Negotiation Strategy
- The interview has a **dual purpose**: sell yourself *and* assess whether the job/company is right for you
- **Salary disclosure strategy**: avoid stating current or desired salary; use phrases like *"I'd prefer to focus on the value I can bring rather than my current salary."*
- **Base salary focus**: negotiate base salary only — bonuses and stock are unpredictable in value
- **Counter-offer strategy**: a good counter is **10–20% above** the initial offer
- Negotiation phases: **Preparation → Post-interview follow-up → Initial offer → Counter → Prep for final discussion → Final discussion**

### 3. Justifying a Raise or Promotion
- A raise is justified when your **value has increased** since your salary was last set
- Companies promote on **proven results, not potential**
- If denied: ask your manager for a **plan to reach the goal** and start developing the needed skills

### 4. Negotiation Mindset
- **Walk-away confidence**: ending without a job is a positive outcome — better than an underpaid acceptance
- Confidence comes from **preparing your case well** and knowing your worth

### 5. Communication Principles
- Use **non-confrontational, value-focused language** in all salary discussions
- Example: *"I want this move to be a big step forward for me in both responsibility and compensation."*
