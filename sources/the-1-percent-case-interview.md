## Summary

A 294-page insider's guide to consulting case interviews by a former McKinsey consultant. Rejects framework memorization in favor of First Principles thinking + MECE deconstruction. Introduces the SCORE Framework for personal fit interviews, the Pyramid Principle for top-down communication, and the 9-Step Math Approach for quantitative reasoning. Covers McKinsey-specific formats (Problem Solving Interview, PEI), the 80/20 preparation strategy, and the "airport test" for cultural fit.

## Core Message

> Only 1% of applicants receive offers from top consulting firms. The difference isn't knowledge — it's how you think, structure, and communicate. Ditch memorized frameworks; master First Principles thinking.

## Key Takeaways

1. **First Principles > Frameworks**: Deconstruct problems from root causes using MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive)
2. **SCORE Framework** for fit interviews: Situation → Complication → Outcome expectation → Remedial action → End result
3. **Pyramid Principle**: Answer first, then supporting arguments — top-down communication
4. **9-Step Math Approach**: Systematic quantitative reasoning without calculators — directionally correct > perfectly precise
5. **80/20 preparation**: 80-90% individual drills, only 10-20% mock interviews
6. **Fit interview = Case interview**: "Airport test" — would the interviewer want to be stuck with you for 3 hours?
7. **McKinsey specifics**: Problem Solving Interview (interviewer-led) and PEI (Personal Experience Interview) require separate preparation
