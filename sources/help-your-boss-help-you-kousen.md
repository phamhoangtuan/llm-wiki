## Summary

Ken Kousen's *Help Your Boss Help You* (154 pages) is a concise guide to **managing up** — cultivating trust through consistency, ownership, and tactful advocacy. The reader's notes condense nine actionable points for building a productive relationship with your manager without becoming a sycophant.

## The Nine Points

1. **Advise, don't decide** — present options with risks/benefits; let your manager choose
2. **Prioritize timeliness** — deliver "good enough" solutions quickly to build trust
3. **Own outcomes** — proactively solve problems, ask for help when stuck, and follow through
4. **Build loyalty first** — support your manager publicly/privately to earn reciprocal trust
5. **Push back calmly** — address harmful decisions respectfully, then refocus on solutions
6. **Use balanced pushback** — cooperate fully, address issues immediately, then reset
7. **Keep it professional** — avoid oversharing; assume oversights, not malice
8. **Own mistakes** — apologize, fix errors, and share prevention plans
9. **Be indispensable** — prioritize reliability, team alignment, and clear communication

Core theme: **cultivate trust through consistency, ownership, and tactful advocacy.**
