## Summary

Reid Hoffman's *The Start-Up of You* (312 pages) applies start-up strategy to the individual career: treat yourself as a company, embrace "permanent beta," and invest continuously in your competitive advantage. The book's guidelines, condensed by the reader into ten points, form a career-management system built on ABZ planning and relationship capital.

## The Ten Guidelines

1. **Embrace "permanent beta"** — treat your career as ongoing growth: learn, adapt, and evolve continuously
2. **Specialize, don't generalize** — excel in your strengths rather than chasing universal superiority
3. **Align assets, aspirations, and market realities** — balance these three dynamically as they shift
4. **Boost competitive advantage** — strengthen skills *or* position yourself where your existing assets outperform others
5. **Act first, iterate with ABZ planning** — prioritize actionable experiments (Plan A/B) with recoverable failures; keep Plan Z as a safety net
6. **Pivot adjacently for Plan B** — move into niches related to your current work, leveraging existing skills and relationships incrementally
7. **Secure Plan Z** — maintain a fallback option to mitigate fear of failure and enable bold risks
8. **Invest in relationships, not just networks** — prioritize quality connections, reciprocity, and proactive maintenance
9. **Seize risky, people-centric opportunities** — commit to ambiguous but promising paths; evaluate *people*, not just roles
10. **Synthesize critically** — filter advice, discard noise, and weigh insights by relevance and credibility
