## Summary

Jordan Cutler, Staff Engineer at Pinterest, shares his journey from Senior to Staff through a case study in Gregor Ojstersek's forthcoming book *The Multiplier Mindset*. The transition isn't about deeper technical skills — it's about three dimensions: expanding your surface area, influencing across teams, and building scalable systems. Concrete examples from Pinterest include creating the #how-i-ai Slack channel (1200+ members), performance audits across surfaces driving 30%+ improvements, and building a developer experience measurement framework that runs without him.

## Core Message

> Staff engineering isn't about doing more of what you did as a Senior. It's about building things where other people do more because of what you built. A Slack channel that answers its own questions. A performance audit that gives a team the data to fix their own page. A measurement framework that runs while you're on vacation. That's the work that compounds.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Three dimensions of Staff Engineering**: Expand surface area (create spaces, seed value, let them compound), influence across teams (don't just identify problems — offer to fix them with data), build scalable systems (frameworks others can hook into)
2. **Don't wait to be assigned**: Create the #how-i-ai channel without being asked → led to exec demos, AI Pathfinders group, company-wide standards
3. **Reduce friction to drive action**: "Your page is slow" gets ignored. "Your page is slow, here's the data, here's the fix, I'll help" gets action
4. **Metrics over vibes**: Built RAG thresholds for DX metrics — CI <12 min = green, 12-18 = yellow, >18 = red. Concrete thresholds create urgency
5. **Compounding systems**: Declarative config (one file per team) → automated weekly reports → self-sustaining. New teams onboard in a day.
