## Summary

Gergely Orosz investigates the rapid collapse of Meta's legendary engineering culture, documenting how leadership — driven by AI obsession — forcefully reassigned 30-50% of core engineers to data labeling, deployed invasive keystroke/mouse tracking, measured token usage in performance reviews, and gutted infra/security teams. The result: a catastrophic Instagram account takeover outage (zero-auth password reset via AI-generated code with no human review), the CISO's immediate resignation, and a mass exodus of tenured engineers.

## Core Message

> Meta's engineering culture is dead because leadership has made it clear that engineering at the company is a cost center — not the profit center it was for 20 years. AI psychosis, not AI itself, destroyed one of Silicon Valley's most admired engineering orgs.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Two eras of Meta culture**: "Move fast and break things" (2010s, legendary) → "Move fast with stable infra" (early 2020s, mature) → now: AI psychosis
2. **Forced reassignments**: 30-50% of engineers on core teams drafted into ADO (Agent Data Optimisation) for full-time data labeling and RLHF — 4,500-5,000 of ~25,000 engineers affected
3. **Keystroke tracking**: Every keystroke and mouse click logged for AI training data generation; no opt-out (later partially walked back after employee backlash)
4. **Tokenmaxxing**: Token usage measured in performance reviews → engineers overuse AI to boost personal stats → AI-generated code with AI-only reviews becomes normalized
5. **Instagram takeover outage (30 May 2026)**: AI-generated, AI-reviewed code enabled zero-auth password reset; Trust & Safety team gutted 50% → CISO resigned next day
6. **PSC weaponization**: Meta's hyper-aggressive performance review (PSC) incentivizes optimizing all measured metrics, including token count, at the expense of real work
7. **Scale AI playbook**: Alexandr Wang (Scale AI CEO, brought in via $14.8B stake acquisition) applying Scale's data labeling factory model to Meta's engineering workforce
8. **Mitchell Hashimoto's warning**: "AI psychosis" — founders over-estimating AI capabilities, casting aside sensible safeguards, repeating the MTBF-vs-MTTR lesson infrastructure learned during cloud transition
9. **Engineering as cost center**: Leadership's actions communicate that building a coding LLM matters more than operating Instagram/Facebook/WhatsApp reliably
10. **Talent exodus**: interviewing.io data shows massive spike in interview prep signups from Meta engineers in May 2026
