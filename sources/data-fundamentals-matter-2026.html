In 2026 The Data Fundamentals Matter More Than Ever

By Ben Rogojan (SeattleDataGuy)article2026-06-13Ingested: 2026-06-15
data-engineeringfundamentalscareeraisqlpython

Summary

Ben Rogojan (SeattleDataGuy) argues that despite the AI hype cycle of 2026, the fundamentals of data engineering — SQL, Python, data modeling, and "glue technical skills" — matter more than ever. The article draws parallels to the culinary world ("a little solid technique") and warns that skipping foundations in favor of AI tools will lead to a massive data mess, undermining data governance and the DataOps discipline that keeps pipelines reliable.
