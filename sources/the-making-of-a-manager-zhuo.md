## Summary

Julie Zhuo's *The Making of a Manager* — read in the Vietnamese edition (**Bí quyết trở thành nhà quản lý tài ba**, 285 pages) — distills her journey from new manager to VP of Product Design at Facebook into eight memorable core principles for anyone leading a team. The book is practical, anecdote-driven, and oriented around the manager's real job: helping the *team*, not doing the work yourself.

## The Eight Core Principles

1. **Focus on the "Team" instead of the "Individual"**
   - Evaluate based on *team* results, not your own output — never do the work yourself
   - Build the belief that success comes from collective effort

2. **Empower and stretch potential**
   - Help people solve problems *themselves* (don't hand them ready-made advice)
   - Delegate authority to capable people and stretch their abilities
   - Hire talented people to multiply the team's impact

3. **Build a safe, caring environment**
   - Genuinely care about employees: understand their concerns, help them succeed
   - Communicate positively: highlight strengths, show goodwill and support
   - Create an open environment for discussion and disagreement

4. **Develop people through feedback**
   - 1:1s centered on the employee: what would help them succeed more
   - Ask for feedback from your own manager (treat them as a coach)
   - Help people drop bad habits and widen their influence

5. **Adapt and keep learning**
   - Adaptability is a survival trait for managers
   - Asking for help is courage, not weakness
   - Invest in your own development for the shared future

6. **Remove performance barriers**
   - Poor performance stems from *lack of skill* or *lack of motivation* — diagnose which
   - Be frank with low performers / bad behaviors; if needed, let people go

7. **Protect team values**
   - Never compromise with bullying or toxic behavior
   - Cultivate healthy manager–employee relationships

8. **Lead with vision and a growth mindset**
   - Motivating others to cooperate on problems is what makes you a leader
   - Growth mindset: seek the truth, treat change as opportunity
