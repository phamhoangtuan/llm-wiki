## Summary

Edmond Lau's *The Effective Engineer* (296 pages) is a concise guide to **making a disproportionate and meaningful impact** in software engineering by concentrating effort on high-leverage work. The reader's notes distill nine steps covering leverage, prioritization, compounding skills, validation, estimation, simplification, team amplification, metrics, and reflection.

## The Nine Steps

1. **Focus on high-leverage work** — automate repetitive tasks; prioritize projects with scalable impact
2. **Ruthlessly prioritize** — allocate 20% time to strategic goals using the "Important vs. Urgent" framework
3. **Invest in compounding skills** — learn system design, debugging; stretch yourself with challenging projects
4. **Validate early, pivot fast** — test ideas via prototypes/A-B tests; kill unviable projects quickly
5. **Estimate with milestones** — tackle riskiest tasks first, set measurable goals, avoid burnout
6. **Simplify and automate** — avoid over-engineering; script repetitive workflows; design resilient systems
7. **Amplify team impact** — mentor others, document processes, and share credit for wins
8. **Track key metrics** — measure business impact, learning velocity, and efficiency gains
9. **Review weekly** — reflect on progress, adjust strategies, and refine habits

Core principle: **balance high-impact execution with continuous learning and team collaboration.**
