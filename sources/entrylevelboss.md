# #ENTRYLEVELBOSS

**Author:** Alexa Shoen | **Type:** Ebook | **Pages:** 298

## Summary

The #ENTRYLEVELBOSS Job Search System is a structured 9-step methodology designed to transform "panicking job seekers" into "hired, happy professionals." Built on the philosophy that job seekers must become the "CEO of their own career," it is divided into four phases:

**Phase 1: Mental Reframing** — Debunks 14 incorrect beliefs about employment (e.g., a degree guarantees a job). Teaches that employers are imperfect humans "making it up on the fly," lowering the psychological barrier to outreach.

**Phase 2: Preparation (Steps 1–5)** — Tactical foundation: defining a target employer list, "skill hacking" (closing small skill gaps via self-learning), creating a "BuzzFeed-ified" skimmable CV, and cover letters that tell a compelling story.

**Phase 3: Strategic Networking (Steps 6–8)** — Leverages the "Theory of Weak Ties" (acquaintances as bridge-builders). Tiered outreach templates for personal networks, target companies, and "Magical Sparks" (strangers). Core tenet: make it "stupid-easy" for contacts to help.

**Phase 4: Execution and Habit (Step 9)** — Reframes the job search as a marketing funnel. Consistent high-volume outreach with acceptance that only a small percentage converts. Emphasizes "dream employee" behaviors before being hired.

## Key Claims

- Networking (especially weak ties) is the most effective way to secure a job
- Traditional "official experience" is overrated; skill gaps can be hacked
- The CV should be highly skimmable — "BuzzFeed-ified"
- Success comes from "Rinse and Repeat," not a silver bullet
- Always have a "next move" to stay unstuck
